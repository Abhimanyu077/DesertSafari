Booking FAQs

Common questions about the booking process

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking at least 2-3 days in advance during peak season (October-March). During off-season, same-day bookings are often possible. For groups of 10+, please book at least a week ahead.

Is there a cancellation policy? +

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the tour. Cancellations within 24-48 hours will incur a 50% charge. No-shows are non-refundable. We're flexible with rescheduling - just let us know!

Do you offer hotel pickup from Jodhpur? +

Yes! We offer pickup and drop from any hotel in Jodhpur city, railway station, or airport. The pickup charge is included in most of our packages. For budget options, you can also arrange your own transport to Osian.

What if the weather is bad on my tour day? +

Safety first! If weather conditions are unsafe (sandstorm, heavy rain), we will reschedule your tour at no extra cost. We monitor weather closely and will contact you in advance if there are any concerns.
