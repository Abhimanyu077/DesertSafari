Camel Safari

⏰ 2 - 6 Hours 💰 From ₹1,500 / person

Experience the timeless charm of the Thar Desert on the back of our gentle, well-trained camels. Traverse golden sand dunes, pass through scrubland, and witness the desert come alive as our expert guides share stories of Rajasthani culture and desert life.

Choose from a short 2-hour sunset ride or an extended 6-hour expedition into the deeper dunes with chai stops and a traditional snack break.

Jeep Safari

⏰ 3 - 5 Hours 💰 From ₹2,500 / person

Buckle up for an adrenaline-pumping 4x4 adventure across the rugged desert terrain. Our experienced drivers navigate through sand dunes, dry riverbeds, and off-road trails that take you to the most spectacular and remote corners of the Thar.

Visit hidden villages, see wildlife like desert foxes and peacocks, and reach dune crests that offer panoramic views of the endless desert landscape.

Desert Camping

⏰ Overnight (1N/2D) 💰 From ₹3,000 / person

The ultimate desert experience! Spend a magical night on the sand dunes under a canopy of a million stars. Our campsite features comfortable tents, traditional Rajasthani bedding, and all the amenities you need for a memorable night.

The evening begins with a camel ride to the campsite, followed by a bonfire, live folk music and dance, and a delicious traditional dinner cooked on open fire. Wake up to a stunning desert sunrise and hot chai.

  • ✓ Comfortable tent stay
  • ✓ Traditional dinner & breakfast
  • ✓ Camel ride to campsite
  • ✓ Bonfire & folk music
  • ✓ Stargazing session
  • ✓ Sunrise & morning chai

Village Tour

⏰ 3 - 4 Hours 💰 From ₹1,200 / person

Immerse yourself in the rich culture and daily life of rural Rajasthan. Visit traditional Bishnoi and Rajput villages where time seems to stand still. Meet local artisans, potters, and weavers practicing crafts passed down through generations.

Enjoy a cup of chai in a local home, watch traditional craft demonstrations, and learn about the desert communities' remarkable relationship with nature.
