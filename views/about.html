About Us

The people behind your unforgettable desert experience

Our Story - Born in the Desert

My name is Robin Singh Rathore, and I was born and raised in the ancient town of Osian. As a child, I would ride camels through the dunes with my grandfather, who would tell me stories of the old caravan routes that once connected Rajasthan to the Silk Road.

In 2009, I decided to share this incredible heritage with travelers from around the world. What started as a simple camel ride operation has grown into a full desert experience company, but our values remain the same - authenticity, safety, and genuine hospitality.

Every tour I lead is personal. I know every dune, every trail, every hidden spring in this desert. When you travel with us, you're not just a tourist - you're our guest, and in Rajasthani tradition, "Atithi Devo Bhava" - the guest is God.

What We Stand For

The principles that guide every experience we create

💚

Authentic Culture

We showcase real Rajasthani life - not a performance. Our village visits, food, and music are all genuine.

🐫

Animal Welfare

Our camels are family. They are well-fed, rested, and never overworked. We follow strict ethical animal care practices.

🌿

Eco-Friendly

We practice responsible tourism. Minimal plastic, zero litter policy, and solar-powered camp lighting.

🤝

Community First

We employ local guides, source food locally, and ensure tourism benefits reach the desert communities.
