Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your desert adventure

What is the best time to visit Osian for a desert safari? +

The best time is October to March when the weather is pleasant (15-30°C). Avoid the summer months (April-June) when temperatures can exceed 45°C. The monsoon season (July-September) occasionally brings rain which creates a unique green desert landscape.

How do I reach Osian from Jodhpur? +

Osian is just 65 km from Jodhpur (about 1 hour by car). We offer pickup from Jodhpur city, railway station, or airport. You can also reach by local bus or hire a private taxi.

Is the camel safari safe for children? +

Yes! Our camels are gentle and well-trained. Children above 3 years can ride with a parent. We also have shorter safari options perfect for families with young kids.

What is included in the desert camping package? +

Our camping package includes: tent accommodation, traditional Rajasthani dinner & breakfast, camel ride, bonfire, folk music & dance performance, and morning chai with sunrise view. Bedding, blankets, and basic toiletries are provided.

Do you offer customized tour packages? +

Absolutely! We specialize in creating customized experiences. Whether you want a romantic couple's getaway, a family adventure, or a photography-focused tour, we tailor everything to your preferences and budget.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept cash (INR), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and card payments. A 20% advance is required to confirm your booking, with the balance payable on the day of the tour.
